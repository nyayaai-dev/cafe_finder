        About this finder

        Every named cafe and restaurant from Colaba to Mira Road and Bhayandar, Thane, Bhiwandi, Kalyan and Dombivli, and Navi Mumbai to Panvel, on one map.

        • Places and opening hours come from OpenStreetMap, refreshed on the 1st of every month. Last update: 16 November 2026.
        • Ratings and cost for two for popular areas come from the listing named on each place (Zomato, Swiggy, EazyDiner, District, Justdial and others). Sites often disagree, so treat them as a guide.
        • Pure veg, Jain and non-veg show only where the data says so. Non-veg is sometimes inferred from the cuisine (seafood, kebab, biryani).
        • Open now is worked out from listed hours in Mumbai time; places without hours aren't shown as open or closed.
        • Moods such as Date night or Work-friendly are best guesses from cuisine, cost and chain names.

        Privacy

        • No accounts, no ads and no tracking cookies.
        • Saved places stay in this browser only. Clearing site data removes them.
        • Near me uses your location only on your phone, to sort by distance. It is never sent anywhere.
        • Directions, menus and ratings open Google Maps, Zomato or Swiggy, which have their own privacy rules.
