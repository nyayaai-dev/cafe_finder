Privacy

Short version: this site doesn't know who you are and doesn't want to.

What stays on your device

  • Saved places (♥) are kept in your browser's storage. They never leave your phone or computer, and clearing site data removes them.
  • Near me asks your browser for your location and uses it only on your device, to sort places by distance and show walking times. It is never sent to us or stored.
  • The site caches its own files so it works offline after the first visit.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-ins.
  • No ads, and no advertising or tracking cookies.
  • No selling or sharing of data.

Visitor counts

If visitor counting is switched on, it uses GoatCounter, which doesn't use cookies or collect personal data.

Other sites

Directions, menus and ratings open Google Maps, Zomato, Swiggy and other sites, which have their own privacy policies. “Suggest a place” and “Report a problem” open GitHub, where whatever you submit is public.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security.
